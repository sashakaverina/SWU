Support Ukraine

Stand With Ukraine Japan works in close coordination with the volunteers and NGOs already inside Ukraine, and delivers your donation directly to the people who need it.

Donations are used to procure food, medicines, medical equipment, generators and fuel for transport. To make sure our work is transparent, we publish reports every few days showing the total raised, and when, how much, for what purpose and to whom each delivery went.

In the areas under Russian occupation especially, more and more families cannot get food. Some cannot evacuate at all, because they have no car or cannot obtain fuel. The volunteers on the ground put their own lives at risk, doing what is possible under the current conditions to deliver essential supplies to people who cannot buy or travel for themselves.

So that as much support as possible reaches the children who will inherit the enormous task of rebuilding Ukraine, we would be deeply grateful for your help.
